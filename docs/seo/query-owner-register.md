## Conversion definitions

- `click_to_call`: a visitor clicks a telephone link. This is an intent signal, not a confirmed lead.
- `email_click`: a visitor clicks an email link. This is an intent signal, not a confirmed lead.
- `generate_lead`: the server reports that the enquiry was delivered. This is a delivered website enquiry, not a qualified lead, quote or sale.
- Qualified lead, quote and sale require CRM or manual outcome data and must not be inferred from Google Analytics alone.

## Governance rules

1. Improve an existing primary owner before proposing a new indexable URL.
2. Keep page titles, H1s, copy and internal-link anchors aligned with the owner role.
3. Keep entity and insurance facts on About; service evidence stays on the relevant commercial owner.
4. Record Google-selected URLs separately from this governance assignment.
5. Do not publish rankings, attribution or response-time promises without current evidence.
6. Current customer response standard: reply within 24 hours, confirmed by the business owner on 17 August 2026.
